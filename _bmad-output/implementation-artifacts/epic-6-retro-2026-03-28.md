### Patterns to Document (Action Item #2)

1. **`Ref.update` post-`uploadPart` timing**: `Ref.update` fires after `uploadPart` resolves — polling `getProgress()` inside `uploadPart` for part 1 sees 0 bytes. Poll must target part > 1 to observe progress.
2. **`TestClock.adjust` for time-based Schedule tests**: `Schedule.exponential` and other time-dependent schedules require `Effect.fork` + `TestClock.adjust("Xms")` to advance virtual time in tests. Without this, tests wait real milliseconds. `Schedule.recurs` (no delays) works without this pattern.
3. **`normalizeCallback` + `mapError` double-wrapping**: When an Effect-typed callback is passed to `uploadPart`, the error goes through both `normalizeCallback`'s try/catch and `mapError`, resulting in double-wrapping. Use raw `throw` or `Promise.reject` in test callbacks to accurately simulate the real user scenario.
