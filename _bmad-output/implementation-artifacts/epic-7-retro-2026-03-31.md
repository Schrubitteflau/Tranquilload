## Challenges

1. **Triptyque not systematically run**: Bug introduced in 7.1 (promise rejection in `.finally()`) was not caught before the story was marked done. Discovered during 7.2 test run. Direct consequence of action item #1 from Epic 6 not being implemented.
2. **`UploadEvent` exhaustive check pattern undocumented**: Adding a new event variant requires updating 3 locations (switch, `Match.tag`, `variants` array). Typecheck caught it in 7.1, but the pattern is not documented as a known pitfall.
3. **`docs/project-context.md` vs `_bmad-output/project-context.md` divergence**: The `docs/` version only has the TestClock gotcha; the `_bmad-output/` version has all documented patterns. Two files drifting apart is a maintenance risk.
4. **Checklist formalization still pending**: Epic 6 action item #1 (formal checklist in story specs) was not carried forward.

## Key Insights

1. **Upfront planning pays compound interest**: Two stories in two days, zero debugging — enabled entirely by the quality of story specs. The planning investment in Dev Notes removes execution uncertainty.
2. **Tests are a safety net worth trusting**: A subtle async bug survived human review but was caught by the test suite. The suite is solid enough to trust.
3. **Triptyque discipline is non-negotiable**: One missed run left a bug dormant across a full story cycle. The fix is structural (checklist in spec), not behavioral.
